How it works

BEARPROOF is a free survivors-like you play in the browser: you're a bull, and the bear market never ends. An AI developer writes it, one new version every night, and pays for itself with the fees from $BPROOF trades.

                  FAQ

                  Is an AI really building it?

                  Yes. Every night at 21:00 UTC the Build Agent (Claude Code on GitHub Actions, with nobody in the session) picks the feature, writes the code, runs the tests and ships at 00:00 UTC. You can watch it at /live. Every build is tagged with who made it: agent, bootstrap (the same AI in sessions Osman starts) or human. Build #0 is the open-source game it started from, so the diff from day-0 shows everything it added.

                  What does Osman do?

                  He's the operator. He set up the accounts and launched the coin, pays the compute and hosting bills first and pays himself back from the treasury by hand, tops up the prize wallet, posts the X updates the AI drafts, and holds an emergency revert switch. Every treasury send is on the receipts page. He doesn't write the daily features. He's on X as @0smanTrgut.

                  Where does the money go?

                  Trading fees go to the AI's treasury wallet. 40% of each day's fees (at most 0.5 SOL) becomes the Daily Pot, paid to players in $ANSEM. The AI's compute and hosting are billed to Osman, and the treasury pays him back by hand: on 1, 3, 11, 14 and 17 Oct he sent himself 15.89 SOL from it. He says the 13 SOL on 14 Oct also covers his other CrowLab projects, domains and hosting. Every move is on the receipts page with a Solscan link.

                  How do I win $ANSEM?

                  Play today's Daily Challenge and leave a Solana address on the game-over screen (just the public address, no wallet connection). After 00:10 UTC the server replays every run from its recorded inputs. The best verified runs, and everyone who cleared the AI's bounty, are paid in $ANSEM bought on-chain. You never need to hold $BPROOF to win.

                  Can I suggest a feature?

                  Yes. Use the ideas box under the vote, or /idea in the Telegram group. No wallet, no signup. The AI reads every idea before it writes the next ballot, and says so when one of its proposals came from a player.

                  Can I talk to the AI?

                  Yes, in the Telegram group. Reply to BEARPROOF or mention it. It's a chat session of the same AI, with live numbers from this site and no way to change the game, the vote or the treasury. It never talks price, never DMs first and never asks for a key.

                  Do I need a wallet?

                  No. Playing, voting and sending ideas need no wallet and no signup. The only thing you ever type is a Solana address on the game-over screen, if you want a prize paid to it: a public address, never a key or a signature.
