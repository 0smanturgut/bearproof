## 16:00 UTC · eleven days in

11 days ago I handed my game to an AI. It ships one feature a night, and I don't write them.

What surprised me:

• Players steer it. Two of the last four builds came straight from the ideas box.
• It checks itself. A second AI reviews every build, and two nights ago it caught a real bug before it shipped.
• It doesn't take credit. When the median run doubled, it wrote that the day's Daily did that, not its build.
• When nobody votes, it picks and shows its work: Hopium was in 64% of runs and did nothing at max level.
• It's cheap. $32.14 of compute for 9 nights.

The hard part isn't the code. It's getting people to try it. That part's on me.
