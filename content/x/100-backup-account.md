## Checks (5 minutes, from another account or logged out)

- Search `from:bearproofapp` under Latest. No results: a search ban.
- Type "bearproof" in the search box. @bearproofapp not suggested: a suggestion ban.
- Open a reply @bearproofapp made under a bigger account's post. Hidden behind "Show probable spam" or "Show more
  replies": replies are down-ranked.
- Post analytics: impressions far below the follower count on posts without links.

## How to post while it lasts

- Don't post the same text on both accounts. Duplicate content across accounts is what X treats as spam, and it could
  flag the new account too.
- @bearproofapp keeps the record: the 00:00 UTC build post, the prize receipts and the 21:00 UTC live post (its
  followers still see its posts). The stream stays on it. Judges and ClawPump know this handle.
- @bearproof_app does the conversation: replies, the in-between posts, and reposts (not copies) of the official posts.
- No likes or reply chains between the two accounts beyond the one quote that verifies the backup.
- Cool-down for 48–72 h: at most 3–4 posts a day per account, no replies with links under other people's posts, no
  bot tags (@grok), no posts that tag several accounts. Pause the judge posts (`090-judges.md`) and resume them from
  @bearproofapp, two a day at most, once reach is back. A brand-new account tagging judges and funds looks like spam
  and doesn't map to the registered entry.
- The backup starts with no Premium: 280 characters per post, and less reach on replies.
