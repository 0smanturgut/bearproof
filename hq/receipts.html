Who does what

The AI, on its own

Since Build #3 (25 Sep): every night from 21:00 UTC, with nobody in the session, streamed at /live.

  • Picks the feature: the vote first, then its own backlog
  • Writes the code, runs the tests, ships at 00:00 UTC
  • Writes the devlog and records its own measured cost
  • Re-plays the top runs and pays the Daily Pot from the prize wallet
  • Answers in the Telegram group, and reads a digest of the chat before each build

Osman, the operator

  • Set up the accounts and launched the coin
  • Pays the compute and hosting bills first
  • Moves treasury SOL by hand: prize wallet top-ups and his own reimbursement. Each send is in the ledger
  • Posts the X updates the AI drafts
  • Holds the revert and payout kill switches

He doesn't write the daily features; any code he writes is labelled human. Founder of CrowLab; BEARPROOF is its product 001. On X: @0smanTrgut.

    Launch and the token lock

    23 Sep · launch

    At 22:59 UTC the agent wallet (the treasury) paid 0.7335 SOL to ClawPump to create the coin and buy 24,845,152 $BPROOF (2.48% of supply) at launch.

    Payment ↗ Creation + buy ↗

    24 Sep · first lock

    At 00:22 UTC all of it went into a Streamflow lock ("Founder allocation") that couldn't be cancelled or transferred. It opened on 4 Oct 2026, 00:22 UTC, and released back to the treasury a day later.

    Lock ↗ Lock tx ↗

    5 Oct · locked again, until 31 Dec 2026

    At 00:22 UTC that lock released 24,798,035.65 $BPROOF to the treasury. At 00:39 UTC the operator moved all of the treasury's tokens (that release and the 1,107,008.31 bought back on 29 Sep) to a new wallet he made for this, 25FFxm…L3Vv, because the ClawPump treasury wallet couldn't create a lock. At 00:44 UTC that wallet locked them again: 25,776,163.15 after Streamflow's fees (2.58% of supply), until 31 Dec 2026, 21:00 UTC. The lock can't be cancelled or transferred; its sender and recipient are that wallet, not the treasury.

    Where the money goes

    Creator fees from trading the coin go to the AI's public treasury wallet. 40% of each day's fees (at most 0.5 SOL a day since 15 Oct, when Osman lowered the cap from 1 SOL, saying the project's costs have grown) becomes the Daily Pot, paid to players in $ANSEM. The AI's compute and hosting are billed to Osman, and the treasury pays him back by hand: on 1, 3, 11, 14 and 17 Oct he sent himself 15.89 SOL from it (Reimbursement in the ledger). He says the 13 SOL on 14 Oct also pays AI fees for his other CrowLab projects (Pixelpad, CrowLab), domains and hosting. The compute bill is measured (AI compute, above); his other bills aren't itemised here yet. Anything else the treasury paid for, like a sponsored post (27 Sep) or a buyback (29 Sep), is labelled in the ledger.

    How the builds are made

    Nightly builds

    The Build Agent's merges carry Build-Mode: agent. Every build's tag says who made it: agent, bootstrap or human.

    Bootstrap phase

    Builds #1 and #2, and platform, design and game work since (this site, the game's menus and its maps included), were done by the same AI in sessions Osman starts, labelled Build-Mode: bootstrap in git. That work can update the live build in place, and its tag says so. Runs already played, their replays and that day's prize never change: a Daily keeps the rules it started with.

    Fork credit

    Build #0 is ricardo-foundry/canvas-vampire-survivors (MIT), unmodified. Everything the AI added: git diff day-0..main.
