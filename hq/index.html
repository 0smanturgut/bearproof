      08

      FAQ

      Is the AI really building it?

      Yes, and you can check. Every change is a public commit labelled with who made it: agent (the scheduled Build Agent: Claude Code running on GitHub Actions), bootstrap (the same AI in sessions Osman starts, used to set up the pipeline), or human (Osman typed it). Build #0 is the untouched open-source game the AI started from, so the diff from day-0 shows everything the AI added.

      What does Osman do?

      He's the operator. He created the accounts, pays for hosting until the treasury can, launches the coin, posts the daily X update the AI drafts, moves SOL from the treasury to the prize and costs wallets by hand (each move is in the ledger), and holds an emergency revert switch. He doesn't write the daily features.

      Where does the money go?

      Creator fees from trading the coin go to the AI's public treasury wallet. It pays for the AI's compute and hosting, and a capped daily prize for the Daily Challenge winner. Every inflow and outflow is in the ledger with a Solscan link, and each number is labelled measured or estimate.

      Why $ANSEM prizes?

      The verified #1 of each Daily Challenge gets a small prize paid by the AI from its treasury, in $ANSEM bought on-chain. The prize is capped by the previous day's fees. Holding our coin is never required to win: it's a game prize, not a lottery.

      How do I win the daily prize?

      Play today's Daily Challenge. Your run goes on the board the moment it ends. On the game-over screen, add a Solana address (just the public address, no wallet connection). After 00:00 UTC the AI re-plays the top runs from their recorded inputs, and the best verified run with an address is paid. Prizes start once the prize wallet is funded.

      Can I suggest a feature?

      Yes. Holders with at least 100,000 $BPROOF can post one request a day (until 18:00 UTC) and it goes on the same ballot as the AI's three proposals. If it gets the most votes, the AI builds it next and credits you in the devlog. Requests describe a change to the game; links and anything about keys, wallets or payouts are refused.

      Is it safe to connect my wallet?

      You only need a wallet to vote or post a request. Connecting asks you to sign a plain text message: no transaction, no approval, no funds move. We read your token balance to weight your vote. Playing never needs a wallet, and the prize address is typed in, never connected.

      Are you bearproof?

      A new build ships every day at 00:00 UTC. Play today's challenge, then come back tomorrow and see what the AI built overnight.
