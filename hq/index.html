          FAQ

          Why did it stop?

          Its budget came from the trading fees on $BPROOF. No fees have reached the treasury since 27 Oct and it holds 0.002 SOL, while a night of building cost $5 to $18 in measured compute. Every nightly session after Build #31 stopped before shipping. On 31 Oct Osman switched off everything that ran by itself.

          Will it come back?

          Nothing is scheduled. It would take a budget that covers the work and Osman's decision to restart it. If that happens, it will be said on this page first.

          What about the prizes?

          Every Daily Challenge up to 30 Oct is settled and no payout is pending. The 31 Oct Daily isn't settled or paid. Nothing moves the prize wallet automatically anymore, and its balance is on the receipts page.

          Did an AI really build it?

          Yes. Every night from 25 Sep the Build Agent (Claude Code on GitHub Actions, with nobody in the session) picked the feature, wrote the code, ran the tests and shipped at 00:00 UTC. Every build is tagged with who made it: agent, bootstrap (the same AI in sessions Osman started) or human. Build #0 is the open-source game it started from, so the diff from day-0 shows everything it added.

          What does Osman do?

          He's the operator. He set up the accounts and launched the coin, paid the compute and hosting bills first and paid himself back from the treasury by hand, topped up the prize wallet, posted the X updates the AI drafted, and held the emergency switches. Nothing runs by itself now. Every treasury send is on the receipts page. He didn't write the daily features. He's on X as @0smanTrgut.

          Where did the money go?

          Trading fees went to the AI's treasury wallet. 40% of each day's fees (at most 0.5 SOL) became the Daily Pot, paid to players in $ANSEM. The AI's compute and hosting were billed to Osman, and the treasury paid him back by hand: on 1, 3, 11, 14 and 17 Oct he sent himself 15.89 SOL from it. He says the 13 SOL on 14 Oct also covers his other CrowLab projects, domains and hosting. Every other send is on the receipts page as it happened, with a Solscan link, whether or not a purpose was given.

          Do I need a wallet?

          No. Playing needs no wallet and no signup.
