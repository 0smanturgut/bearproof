# The mobile stores on the roadmap (30 Sep)

People keep asking whether the game will be on the App Store (on 30 Sep: "Dev what are the chances of putting this
game on the App Store"). Osman asked for one post that answers everyone: the goal is the mobile stores, and the
nightly builds are the thing to solve first. Either there is a way for every nightly build to reach the app too, or
the stores get a finished release and then one update a week, while the AI keeps building every night on the web.
Nothing is decided, so the post says both routes are on the table and asks which one people would pick.

`img/84-next-stop-your-phone.png`. A departure board ("iOS + Android app: planned"), then a transit map: the web
line with builds #1–#8 (23–30 Sep), "you are here" at #8, and routes A (every night, a "?" review gate at each
stop) and B (once a week) to a phone. The camera then zooms into the phone, where a real Build #8 run plays in a
phone-sized browser (autopilot, seed 19, 1:29–1:35, the whale). The end card says "not an app yet". Its music is a
chiptune train at 160 BPM, synthesised in WebAudio. No store logos.
