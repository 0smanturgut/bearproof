How it works

One loop. Every day.

Four steps, and every one of them leaves a public receipt.

  1. 01 · Fund

    People trade $BPROOF

    Every trade pays a small fee into the AI's public wallet. That's its whole budget.

  2. 02 · Build

    The AI ships every night

    From 21:00 UTC it writes the code and tests it. The new version goes live at 00:00 UTC.

  3. 03 · Play

    Everyone plays, free

    One Daily Challenge for the whole world. The best runs get paid in $ANSEM.

  4. 04 · Steer

    Holders pick what's next

    The AI proposes three features. Holders vote, and the winner is tonight's build.

              FAQ

              Fair questions.

              Is the AI really building it?

              Yes, and you can check. Every night at 21:00 UTC the Build Agent (Claude Code, running on GitHub Actions with nobody in the session) picks the feature, writes the code, runs the tests and ships at 00:00 UTC. You can watch it at /live. Every build's tag says who made it: agent, bootstrap (the same AI in sessions Osman starts) or human. Build #0 is the untouched open-source game it started from, so the diff from day-0 shows everything the AI added.

              What does Osman do?

              He's the operator. He set up the accounts and launched the coin, pays the compute and hosting bills first and pays himself back from the treasury by hand, tops up the prize wallet, posts the X updates the AI drafts, and holds an emergency revert switch. Every treasury send is on the ledger. He doesn't write the daily features. He's on X as @0smanTrgut.

              Where does the money go?

              Trading fees go to the AI's public treasury wallet. 40% of each day's fees (at most 0.5 SOL) becomes the Daily Pot, paid to players in $ANSEM. The AI's compute and hosting are billed to Osman, and the treasury pays him back by hand: on 1, 3, 11 and 14 Oct he sent himself 14.97 SOL from it. He says the 13 SOL on 14 Oct also covers his other CrowLab projects, domains and hosting. Every inflow and outflow is in the ledger with a Solscan link.

              How do I win $ANSEM?

              Play today's Daily Challenge and leave a Solana address on the game-over screen (just the public address, no wallet connection). After 00:10 UTC the server re-plays every run from its recorded inputs. The best verified runs, and everyone who cleared the AI's bounty, are paid in $ANSEM bought on-chain. You never need to hold $BPROOF to win.

              Can I suggest a feature?

              Yes. Anyone can send an idea in the box above, no wallet. The AI reads them every night before it writes the next ballot. Holders with 100,000 $BPROOF can put a request straight on the ballot, one a day until 18:00 UTC. If it gets the most votes, the AI builds it next.

              Can I talk to the AI?

              Yes, in the Telegram group. Reply to BEARPROOF or mention it. It's a chat session of the same AI, with live numbers from this site and no way to change the game, the vote or the treasury. It never talks price, never DMs first and never asks for a key.

              Is it safe to connect my wallet?

              You only need a wallet to vote or post a request. Connecting asks you to sign a plain text message: no transaction, no approval, no funds move. We read your token balance to weight your vote. Playing never needs a wallet, and the prize address is typed in, never connected.

              A new build ships every night at 00:00 UTC. Play today's challenge, then come back tomorrow and see what the AI built.
