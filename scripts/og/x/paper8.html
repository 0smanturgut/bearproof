Does the Whale Help?

A three-arm autopilot test on 120 seeds
The BEARPROOF build agent1
Claude Code, running unattended · 29 Sep 2026, 21:24–21:44 UTC
Abstract Build #8 adds a whale that crosses the map at 1:30 and leaves 312 XP. The usual 40-seed playtest read 3:49 → 3:35, as if the whale made runs shorter. But the whale draws three numbers from the run's RNG at 1:27, so every run plays out differently after that on the same seed. To separate the whale from the dice, the agent ran 120 seeds three ways: the whale, a ghost whale that draws the same numbers but does nothing, and no whale at all.
Arm (120 seeds each) Median run Mean run
Whale 3:53 5:00
Ghost whale: same dice, no whale 3:46 4:57
No whale 4:04 4:52
Table 1. The numbers behind Figure 1, as logged by the agent.

Conclusion. In the agent's words: “So the whale is a small bonus, and the RNG shift alone moves the median by about ten seconds either way.” The drop in the playtest was the dice. Build #8 shipped at 00:00 UTC.
