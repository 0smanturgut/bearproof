Most asked this week: will BEARPROOF be on the App Store?

Short answer: that's where it's headed, iOS and Android. One thing to solve first: the AI ships a new build every night, and every store update has to wait for review.

So there are two routes, and I'd like your take:

A) Every night
Each build reaches the app the same night, just like on the web. The catch: we need a way to make that work with store review.

B) Once a week
The stores get a finished release first, then one update a week. The web stays nightly, so the AI never slows down.

Which would you pick, A or B?

Until then, it already plays in your phone's browser. No install, no wallet.
