# A paid app that burns the coin: a proposal, open for comment (1 Oct)

Osman's plan, in his words: release the game on the mobile stores as a paid app for a small price, and use what
comes in to buy $BPROOF and burn it. He wants the community's view before building it, and asked for ClawPump to be
tagged. So the post is a proposal with questions, not an announcement.

What the post commits to, and what it leaves open:

- The web game stays free, with the same Daily and the same prizes; neither the app nor the coin is needed to play
  or win. This is the project's standing rule (the coin never gates playing or winning), restated so a paid app is
  not read as a paid entry to the Daily Pot.
- "What's left after the stores' cut and taxes": the stores keep 15–30% of a sale (their published range), and the
  sales are taxable income, so the post promises only the net. Every buy and burn would go on the public ledger.
- No price, no date and no update cadence: none is decided.
- No price talk. The burn is described as what happens to the money, nothing more.

Checked 1 Oct 20:10 UTC: ClawPump's token page now labels our 10% fee mechanic "Buybacks: Buys this token back" (on
30 Sep it read "Buy & burn: Buys this token and burns it"). Five buybacks ran on 1 Oct, 0.0068 SOL in all, and the
mint's supply is 999,999,119.49, so they were not burned. The post therefore doesn't call the fee mechanic a burn,
and asks ClawPump whether outside revenue can go through it and be burned there.

---

I want to put BEARPROOF on the App Store and Google Play as a paid app, for a small one-time price.

And I don't want to keep the money. What's left after the stores' cut and taxes would buy $BPROOF on the open market and burn it.

Before I build it, I want your take.

The plan:
• The web game stays free. Same Daily, same seed, same prizes. You'll never need the app or the coin to play or win.
• The app is the same game on iOS and Android, paid once.
• The stores keep 15–30% of every sale. What's left after that and taxes buys $BPROOF and burns it. Every buy and every burn goes on the public ledger with its transaction.

Why: today the treasury's only income is trading fees. This would be the first money that comes from people playing the game.

Three questions:

1. A paid app that burns, or a free app?
2. If paid, what's a fair price?
3. Burn all of it, or send part of it to the AI's compute bill?

@clawpumptech, I'd like your read too. Would you ship it paid? And could revenue from outside trading go through the token's buyback on ClawPump and be burned there, so everything sits on one public page?

Nothing is built or decided yet. No price promises. It's a plan and a question.
