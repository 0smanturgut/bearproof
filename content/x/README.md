# X drafts

Proof drafts, Osman posts from @bearproofapp. Nothing here is posted automatically.

- `build-<n>.md`: the post for the day Build #n goes live (00:00 UTC). From Build #3 on, the Build Agent writes
  these itself in the same pull request as the build.
- `0NN-*.md`: one-off posts (entry, coin launch, milestones), numbered in posting order.

Format: a note for Osman (when to post, what to attach), then `---`, then the post, then optional `---` replies.
@bearproofapp is Premium, so a post can be long, but only the first ~280 characters show before "Show more": they
have to work on their own. Language rules are in `docs/LAUNCH.md`: builds, players, receipts. Never price.

## X rules we write to (since 25 Sep)

On 25 Sep X limited @bearproofapp's reach: its filters read the launch week's pattern as platform manipulation. What
X's Content Spam policy names, and what we therefore don't do any more:

- **No link-only replies.** "Links shared without commentary" as the bulk of an account's activity is spam to X. A
  link goes inside a post, with context, at most once a day.
- **Few mentions.** No posts tagging several accounts, no tagging bots, no tagging people who haven't engaged with us.
  Replying to someone who mentioned us is fine.
- **No promo replies** under other people's posts.
- **No copypasta.** Vary the wording; no stock "free, no wallet" line on every post.
- **1–3 posts a day** while visibility recovers, then ramp up slowly.
- **One account.** No backup or second accounts: X treats them as evading its enforcement.
